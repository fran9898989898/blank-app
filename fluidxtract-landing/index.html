A funnel wants gravity. That fill hole is on the side.

Differentials, transfer cases and final drives fill from a plug on the side, sometimes underneath — never on top. So you improvise: a bent length of tubing, a turkey baster, a bottle turned upside down and squeezed, racing gravity before your arm gives out. Half of it ends up on the floor, on your sleeve, on the housing you were trying to keep clean.
